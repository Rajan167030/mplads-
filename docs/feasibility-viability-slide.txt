FEASIBILITY & VIABILITY

Technical Feasibility
- 12/12 phases built & verified on real data (9,901 projects, 500 contractors)
- Proven stack: FastAPI, PostgreSQL+PostGIS+pgvector, Next.js, scikit-learn
- Multilingual matching validated - 0.84 similarity score (English to Hindi names)
- Every risk score explainable - not a black box

Economic Feasibility
- 100% open-source stack - zero licensing cost
- Runs on free-tier infra - near-zero cost per constituency
- Works on existing MPLADS CSV exports - no new data infra needed

Social Feasibility
- Flags "investigate," never "fraud" - human stays in control
- Role-based access matches real govt hierarchy
- Public transparency portal - no login needed

Challenges
- Inconsistent source data quality
- Regional-language name matching (romanized text)
- Risk of false positives from naive thresholds
- No live government API yet

Strategies
- Multi-signal fusion - no single false signal decides outcome
- Every flag validated against ground truth before use
- Explainability panel - human verifies, model just points
- Modular ingestion - future live feed plugs in easily

Viability
- Already scaled to 28 states, 9,901 projects
- No re-architecture needed to grow
- Vendor-agnostic AI layer (Gemini/OpenAI/Groq)
- Full audit trail - deployment-ready for govt use
